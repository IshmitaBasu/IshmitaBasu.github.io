01A LITTLE ABOUT ME

The person behind the pipelines.

I like the point where a business question becomes a data problem—and then a solution someone can actually use.

My journey began with client-facing business intelligence at Polestar, where I learned to own projects from requirements to handover. Today, at Uniper Global Commodities, I spend much of my time improving Power BI dashboards for storage, gas and LNG analytics, alongside the SQL and Python work that makes those reports dependable.

Outside work, I’m pursuing an M.Sc. in Data and Knowledge Engineering at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg. My thesis explores MLOps for financial fraud detection—because a useful model should also be testable, reproducible and honest about its limitations.
