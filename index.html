01A LITTLE ABOUT ME

The person behind the reports.

I enjoy taking a reporting problem all the way from “what do we need?” to “this actually works.”

At Polestar Solutions, I owned end-to-end delivery of 200+ Power BI reports, led a project team of 5–8 BI developers and mentored eight new hires. I learned that good analytics includes listening to users, validating the numbers and seeing a report through to handover.

At Uniper Global Commodities, I work on gas storage and LNG analytics, Power BI, SQL and Python. One improvement I proposed and built independently reduced the load time of a Norwegian-model quality dashboard by 60%. I also use AI tools for development, while checking the resulting logic against source data and business rules.

I’m pursuing an M.Sc. in Data and Knowledge Engineering at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg (expected February 2027). My ongoing thesis investigates a reproducible MLOps pipeline for financial fraud detection across five million transactions. I’m now looking for a full-time Data Analyst or Business Intelligence role.
